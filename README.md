## 💎 Our Plugins

### ToxicDiamondFarm v2.0.0 — Stable

**Automated and Upgradeable Diamond Generators for Minecraft Survival Servers!**

Take your survival experience to the next level with a powerful diamond generation system.

**Features:**

- 💎 Automatic Diamond Generation
- ⚙️ Four Upgradeable Generator Levels
- 🖥️ Interactive Upgrade GUI
- 📦 Automatic Chest and Barrel Storage
- 🔒 Generator Ownership Protection
- 🤝 Shared Generator Upgrading
- 🛡️ Explosion and Piston Protection
- 📖 Custom Crafting Recipe
- 💾 Persistent Generator Data

**Production Levels:**

- Level 1 — 1 Diamond / 30 Minutes
- Level 2 — 1 Diamond / 20 Minutes
- Level 3 — 1 Diamond / 10 Minutes
- Level 4 — 1 Diamond / 5 Minutes

**Compatibility:** Paper 26.2 (tested build 123) | Java 25
